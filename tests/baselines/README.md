# Baselines

Recorded outputs from eval runs of the ladder skills, kept as evidence of what each skill
actually produced — not as fixtures a test asserts against.

**These predate 0.6.0.** They were recorded while the `work_item` and `epic` issue forms still
existed, so they talk about filing an "epic" and a "work item". The forms were retired on
2026-09-14 (see `CHANGELOG.md`); the tracking shapes survive under new names, described in
`skills/designing-cldk-changes/references/issue-and-pr-tracking.md`.

They are left unedited on purpose: a baseline is a record of a run, and rewriting one to match
current guidance would falsify it. Re-record them against 0.6.0 before treating any of them as
a current statement of behaviour.
